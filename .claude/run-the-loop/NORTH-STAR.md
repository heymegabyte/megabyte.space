## The defining principle

Megabyte operates around **goals, opportunities, and outcomes — not commands**. A user says
*"Get me more customers"* and Megabyte determines + runs what that needs (research · SEO · pages ·
analytics · experiments · CRM · automation · ads · content · email · social · integrations · DBs ·
agents · scheduled loops · human approvals) — the user never hand-translates outcomes into infra.

**Target experience:** *Tell Megabyte an outcome. It continuously learns, plans, creates, operates,
verifies, improves, and adapts until the outcome is achieved.* The visible UX stays beautiful /
cinematic / fast / calm / obvious / progressively-disclosed / keyboard- & mobile-friendly /
human- & AI-readable. Advanced machinery hides behind intuitive outcomes
("Megabyte found an opportunity that could increase bookings" — never "Durable Object event 18").
